Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need planning permission?

Most garden rooms fall under Permitted Development rights if they meet size and location criteria. We handle this assessment for you.

Are these buildings fully insulated?

Yes, all our garden rooms and annexes are built to building regulations with high-performance insulation suitable for year-round use.

What areas of London do you cover?

We cover all London boroughs. We have regularly worked in Hampstead, Chelsea, Clapham, and Dulwich, but we travel across Greater London.

How long does the build take?

A typical garden room takes 6-8 weeks from foundation to handover. Large annexes or full renovations may take 12-16 weeks.
